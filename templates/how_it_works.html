How GoMyanmar Works

Plan your perfect Myanmar adventure in just a few simple steps

Your Journey in 4 Simple Steps

1

Enter Your Travel Details

Tell us where you want to go, when you're traveling, and how many people are joining. You can choose from popular destinations like Yangon, Mandalay, Bagan, Inle Lake, and more.

  • Select departure and destination cities
  • Choose your travel dates
  • Specify number of travelers
  • Select accommodation type
  • Choose transportation preference
Start Planning
2

Choose Your Perfect Plan

Browse through our curated travel plans tailored to different interests and travel styles. Each plan includes carefully selected activities and experiences.

Cultural Explorer

Perfect for travelers interested in history, temples, and local culture.

Adventure Seeker

For those who love outdoor activities and unique experiences.

Relaxation & Wellness

Slow-paced journeys focused on relaxation and self-care.

3

Customize Your Trip

Review and customize your selected plan. You can:

  • View detailed day-by-day itinerary
  • Check weather forecasts for your travel dates
  • Select specific hotels from our curated list
  • Choose your preferred transportation (bus, flight, etc.)
  • See the complete cost breakdown
4

Confirm & Enjoy Your Trip

Once you're satisfied with your plan, confirm your booking and get ready for your adventure!

  • Confirm hotel and transport bookings
  • Download your detailed itinerary as PDF
  • Print your travel documents
  • Access your trip dashboard anytime
  • Get booking references and confirmations
